Detailed Description

Frame data packet 26: every sensor of the primary that ticks in this frame.

A secondary's mirrors tick on exactly these frames, with these deltas, whatever their own tick interval says, so their sensor_tick phase is the primary's even if they were created later. Written on every frame, even when empty.
