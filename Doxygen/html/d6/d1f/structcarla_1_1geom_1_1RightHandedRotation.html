Detailed Description

Euler angles of a rotation expressed in a right-handed frame, in degrees.

This is the boundary type for ROS / REP-103 "FLU" consumers: x forward, y left, z up, every axis turned by the right-hand rule.

The three angles compose as the usual intrinsic Z-Y-X (yaw, then pitch, then roll) sequence, i.e. R = Rz(yaw) * Ry(pitch) * Rx(roll) with the standard right-handed elementary matrices. That is exactly what scipy.spatial.transform.Rotation.from_euler("xyz", ...), tf2::Quaternion::setRPY and NVIDIA's ClipGT rig.json all mean by "roll pitch yaw".

This is not carla::geom::Rotation. CARLA's frame is left-handed (x forward, y right, z up) and its rotator has mixed signs; the exact mapping is 

right_handed.roll  =  carla.roll
right_handed.pitch = -carla.pitch
right_handed.yaw   = -carla.yaw

which is its own inverse. Use Rotation::ToRightHanded() / Rotation::FromRightHanded() rather than flipping signs by hand, and see Docs/coordinate_conventions.md.
