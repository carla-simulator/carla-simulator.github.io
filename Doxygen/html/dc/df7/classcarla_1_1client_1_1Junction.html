std::vector< SharedPtr< Road > > carla::client::Junction::GetAdjacentRoads ( ) const

The roads outside the junction that meet it.

OpenDRIVE calls these "incoming", but a road has no inherent direction – the same road is an exit for traffic going the other way – so they are adjacent here.
