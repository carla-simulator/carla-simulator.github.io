Detailed Description

C++ port of agents.navigation.constant_velocity_agent.ConstantVelocityAgent.

Drives the ego at a fixed velocity, using BasicAgent's planner just to keep the wheels rotating. A collision sensor is attached on construction and a collision flips the agent into a "stopped" state.
