Detailed Description

Distance pixel format: a single float channel holding a metric distance in METRES.

Produced by the path-traced distance sensor (sensor.camera.rt_lens_distance) as the Euclidean distance from the camera origin to the primary hit along that pixel's lens ray. Rays that hit nothing report a very large value rather than 0.
