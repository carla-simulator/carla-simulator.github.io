28 /// sections, each holding the lanes.
29 ///
30 /// Roads connect end to end through GetNextRoads() / GetPreviousRoads(), or
31 /// through a junction. There is deliberately no left/right here: OpenDRIVE
32 /// gives roads no lateral relation, since a road already contains the lanes
