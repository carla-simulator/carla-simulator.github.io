14/**
15 * A traffic sign baked into a level from the regional sign catalog: a pole and a plate
16 * whose material is a constant instance of the atlas selector with the cell baked in.
17 *
18 * Being an ATrafficSignBase, ATrafficLightManager::SpawnSignals adopts it for the
19 * OpenDRIVE signal it stands at (within 5 m, TrafficSignState agreeing with the signal
20 * type/subtype) and attaches the matching USignComponent, so the map gets the regional
21 * look without a duplicate stock blueprint at the same spot.
