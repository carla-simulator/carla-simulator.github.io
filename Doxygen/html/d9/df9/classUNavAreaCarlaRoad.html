Detailed Description

Nav area stamped on driving-lane surfaces (via the road meshes' nav collision area class).

Expensive to traverse so that crossing pedestrians prefer crosswalks and only cut through open road when the detour is large; excluded entirely from non-crossing walkers' query filters.
