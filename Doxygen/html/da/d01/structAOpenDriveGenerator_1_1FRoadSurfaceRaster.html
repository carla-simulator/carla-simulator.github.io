Detailed Description

The authoritative "what is road surface, where, at what height" tool the rest of generation consumes.

GenerateRoadMesh rasterizes every road/sidewalk triangle (vertices alone leave unconstrained holes inside lanes – the mesh is dense along s but a full lane wide laterally) into a regular XY grid with barycentric-interpolated surface heights, then a chamfer distance transform gives every cell its distance to the driving footprint and to any pavement, plus the height of the nearest pavement. Terrain generation, furniture placement and the generation QA all query this one structure instead of re-deriving geometry their own (subtly different) ways.
