Note
Steering compensation lookup table. Tuples of (desired_steering_angle, actual_steering_angle). Desired steering angle is the normalized steer input applied to the vehicle; actual steering angle is the steady-state tire angle in radians derived from the measured yaw rate via the bicycle model (delta = atan(L * w / v)). Measured on the UE5.8 Chaos Lincoln MKZ (2026-08-20 sweep at ~3 m/s); the response is near-linear, unlike the strongly degressive UE5.5 vehicle the original tier4 table was calibrated against. Only positive values stored; absolute value used for lookup.
