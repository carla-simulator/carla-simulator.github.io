40 /// A vehicle touching the walker while moving at least this fast
41 /// (cm/s) kills it - ue4-dev parity, where any moving-vehicle contact
42 /// was lethal. The threshold only exempts effectively stationary
43 /// vehicles (a walker bumping into a parked or stopped car survives).
44 /// It must stay low: an alive walker's capsule is query-only, so a
45 /// sub-threshold *moving* contact has no physical response at all and
46 /// the car visibly interpenetrates the standing pedestrian.

50 /// How far beyond the capsule radius the vehicle proximity check
51 /// reaches (cm). The margin makes the kill trigger before the first
52 /// physical contact: the capsule is a kinematic body, so to a
53 /// simulating vehicle it is an infinite-mass wall - the impulse of a
54 /// real contact is what used to launch and spin cars. The
55 /// capsule is query-only, so vehicles never physically contact it -
56 /// what launched cars was the wheel suspension raycasts riding the
57 /// blocking capsule like a rigid bollard. The kill therefore fires
58 /// from this proximity poll just before the wheels reach the capsule,
59 /// and the collision event is delivered to the vehicle's sensors
60 /// explicitly from Kill().
61 UPROPERTY(Category="Walker Base", BlueprintReadWrite, EditAnywhere)
62 float VehicleSensorMargin = 50.0f;
