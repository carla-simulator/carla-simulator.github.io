Detailed Description

Internal adapter that holds a Vector3D in a right-handed coordinate frame.

CARLA / Unreal expose vectors in a left-handed frame; Quaternion and Rotation work in right-handed math internally. The Y axis is negated on construction and re-negated on conversion back, so the handedness flip happens exactly once at the boundary.
