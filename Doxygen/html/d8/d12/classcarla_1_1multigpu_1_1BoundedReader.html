Detailed Description

Reads the fields of one packet (or one record inside it) without ever consuming more than its declared size.

A read past the end fails, and a failed reader fails its stream, so the packet loop reports a short packet.

Payload layouts of the Multi-GPU packets:

  • fixed-size records: [u32 count][u16 record_size]{record}
  • variable-size records: [u32 count]{[u32 record_size][record]}
  • optional fields (TLV): {[u8 kind][u32 size][value]} A record or field only ever grows at its end; readers read the fields they know and skip the rest.
