Detailed Description

A stretch of road over which the lane layout does not change.

An OpenDRIVE road is divided along s into lane sections; lanes are added or dropped only at a section boundary. This is why a lane's identity carries a section id, and why Lane::GetNextLanes() can step into a different section.
