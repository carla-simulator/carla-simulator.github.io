Detailed Description

An OpenDRIVE road: a run of carriageway divided along s into lane sections, each holding the lanes.

Roads connect end to end through GetNextRoads() / GetPreviousRoads(), or through a junction. There is deliberately no left/right here: OpenDRIVE gives roads no lateral relation, since a road already contains the lanes on both sides of its centre line. Lateral neighbours live on Lane.
