32 /// C++ port of agents.navigation.global_route_planner.GlobalRoutePlanner.
33 /// Builds a directed graph from carla::client::Map::GetTopology() and runs
34 /// A* search (euclidean heuristic, edge weight = `length`) to produce a
35 /// (waypoint, RoadOption) plan between two world locations.
