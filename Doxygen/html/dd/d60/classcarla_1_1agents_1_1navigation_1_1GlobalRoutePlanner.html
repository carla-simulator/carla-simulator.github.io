Detailed Description

C++ port of agents.navigation.global_route_planner.GlobalRoutePlanner.

Builds a directed graph from carla::client::Map::GetTopology() and runs A* search (euclidean heuristic, edge weight = length) to produce a (waypoint, RoadOption) plan between two world locations.
